## Tips for Using These Templates

- **Personalize the first line** — reference something specific about the company/person before jumping into the pitch
- **Keep subject lines short** — under 8 words performs best for cold outreach
- **Always send the follow-up** — 60% of replies come after the first follow-up
- **Track your sends** — use a simple spreadsheet to log who you contacted, when, and their status
- **Best days to send:** Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 3–5pm Swedish time
- **For LinkedIn:** always try to connect first before sending a message — it's free and has higher reply rates than InMail
