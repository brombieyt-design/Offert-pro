# Offert-pro — Marketing Strategy

**App category:** Quotation & Proposal Software
**Primary audience:** Small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs)
**Date:** March 2026

---

## 1. Positioning Statement

> *Offert-pro helps small businesses close more deals by turning the painful, time-consuming task of writing quotes and proposals into a fast, professional, and trackable workflow — in minutes, not hours.*

### Core value props
| Pain point | Offert-pro solution |
|---|---|
| Quotes made in Word/Excel look unprofessional | Beautiful, branded PDF proposals |
| No idea if a client opened the quote | Real-time open/view tracking |
| Rewriting the same quotes over and over | Reusable templates and product/service catalogs |
| Slow back-and-forth on pricing | In-quote negotiation and e-signature |
| Scattered follow-ups | Automated reminders and status pipeline |

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary segment — SMB owners & managers
- **Who:** Tradespeople, consultants, agencies, accountants, IT service providers, interior designers, event planners, and any service business that sends quotes
- **Size:** 1–50 employees
- **Pain:** Using Word, Excel, or email to send quotes; high quote-to-close time; looks amateur next to competitors
- **Budget:** Willing to pay $20–$80/month for tools that save time and win deals

### Secondary segment — Freelancers
- Independent contractors who send recurring project proposals
- Sensitive to price; freemium or low-cost entry point is critical

### Negative persona (who to avoid early)
- Enterprise sales teams (complex CRM integrations, legal requirements)
- E-commerce / product-only businesses (no need for proposals)

---

## 3. Go-to-Market Phases

### Phase 1 — Awareness & Validation (Months 1–3)
Goal: Get first 100 paying customers, validate messaging, gather testimonials.

**Tactics:**
- **Soft launch on Product Hunt** — post on a Tuesday/Wednesday for max visibility
- **Reddit communities:** r/smallbusiness, r/freelance, r/consulting, r/Entrepreneur — provide genuine value, soft-promote
- **Cold outreach:** target 500 SMB owners in high-quote industries (trades, consulting, events) via LinkedIn
- **Free trial:** 14-day full-feature trial, no credit card required — lower friction to sign up
- **Founding member deal:** First 100 customers get 50% off forever — creates urgency and loyalty

### Phase 2 — Growth & Content (Months 3–9)
Goal: Reach 1,000 MRR customers, build inbound engine.

**Tactics:**
- **SEO content marketing** (see Section 5) — long-term compounding traffic
- **YouTube tutorials:** "How to send professional quotes as a small business" — captures intent-based search
- **Integration partnerships:** connect with accounting tools (QuickBooks, Xero, Wave) and CRMs (HubSpot) — tap into their user bases
- **Affiliate/referral program:** give customers $20 credit per referred paying user
- **Review campaigns:** actively collect G2, Capterra, Trustpilot reviews — critical for SMB buyers

### Phase 3 — Scale (Month 9+)
Goal: Sustainable, predictable growth engine.

**Tactics:**
- **Paid search (Google Ads):** bid on high-intent keywords (see Section 6)
- **Retargeting:** Facebook/Instagram ads targeting trial users who didn't convert
- **Partner with industry associations:** trade unions, chamber of commerce, freelancer platforms (Upwork partner directory)
- **Vertical landing pages:** dedicated pages for plumbers, event planners, IT consultants, etc.

## 4. Pricing Strategy

| Plan | Price | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 / forever | Freelancers; limited to 3 quotes/month |
| **Starter** | $19 / month | Solo SMB owners; unlimited quotes, basic templates |
| **Pro** | $49 / month | Small teams (up to 5 users); tracking, e-signature, integrations |
| **Business** | $99 / month | Growing SMBs (up to 20 users); custom branding, API, priority support |

**Key pricing principles:**
- Free plan generates word-of-mouth ("Powered by Offert-pro" branding on free tier)
- Annual billing discount: 2 months free (reduces churn)
- No per-quote fees — SMBs hate unpredictable billing

## 6. Paid Acquisition

### Google Ads
- **Branded campaigns:** protect brand from competitor bidding
- **Competitor campaigns:** bid on "[Competitor] alternative" and "[Competitor] pricing"
- **Intent campaigns:** "quotation software", "proposal builder", "quote tool online"
- Target CPA: $60–$90 (LTV of a paying SMB customer should be $500+)

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Retarget trial users who didn't convert with case study / social proof ads
- Lookalike audiences based on existing paying customers
- Video ad: "Before Offert-pro vs. After Offert-pro" — 30-second demo

### LinkedIn
- Target job titles: Owner, Director, Operations Manager, Sales Manager
- Company size: 1–50 employees
- Industries: Construction, Consulting, Events, IT Services, Design
- Lead gen form ads: "Get your free proposal template"

## 8. Metrics to Track

### Acquisition
- Monthly sign-ups (free + paid)
- Free-to-paid conversion rate (target: >10%)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) per channel
- Trial activation rate (did they send their first quote?)

### Retention & Revenue
- Monthly churn rate (target: <3%)
- MRR and MRR growth rate
- NPS score (target: >40)
- Average revenue per user (ARPU)

### Engagement
- Quotes sent per active user per month
- Feature adoption (templates, e-signature, tracking)

## 9. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|
| PandaDoc | Expensive, enterprise-focused, complex |
| Proposify | Steep learning curve, costly for solo users |

| Word/Excel | Not a product — our biggest "competitor" by volume |

**Winning angle:** Offert-pro is the fastest, simplest quoting tool built specifically for small businesses — not a stripped-down enterprise tool.
