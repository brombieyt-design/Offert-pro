## 1. SMB & Entrepreneur Associations

### Företagarna (Swedish Federation of Business Owners)
- **Website:** foretagarna.se
- **What:** Sweden's largest independent business owner org — 60,000 member companies. Publishes *Företagaren* magazine.
- **Why relevant:** Their entire member base is Swedish SMB owners — your exact audience.
- **Approach:** Pitch a partnership for member discounts or sponsored content in *Företagaren*. Apply to be a "recommended supplier" in their member benefits program. Sponsor or speak at regional events.

---

### Almega
- **Website:** almega.se
- **What:** Umbrella trade org for ~3,900 service companies — IT consulting, staffing, facilities, media, etc.
- **Why relevant:** Service businesses quote constantly. Sub-associations like Almega Innovationsföretagen (IT/consulting) are power-user segments.
- **Approach:** Reach out to specific sub-associations for co-marketing or member newsletter features.

---

### NyföretagarCentrum
- **Website:** nyforetagarcentrum.se
- **What:** Sweden's leading nonprofit for new business founders — 80+ centers, helps ~16,000 aspiring entrepreneurs/year.
- **Why relevant:** Captures entrepreneurs exactly when they first need quoting tools. Strong word-of-mouth channel.
- **Approach:** Offer a free or discounted starter plan for their clients. Ask to be included in advisor resource lists and onboarding materials.

---

### Handelskammaren (Chamber of Commerce)
- **Website:** handelskammaren.com
- **What:** Private business network for southern Sweden. Regional chambers operate independently in Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö, etc.
- **Why relevant:** Members span construction, consulting, trade, and professional services — all prime quoting tool users.
- **Approach:** Contact regional chapters separately. Sponsor a workshop: *"How to win more business with professional proposals."*

---

### Svenskt Näringsliv (Confederation of Swedish Enterprise)
- **Website:** svensktnaringsliv.se
- **What:** Top-level employers' confederation — 49 member associations, 60,000 companies, 1.6M employees.
- **Why relevant:** Gateway to all major Swedish business associations. Credibility signal.
- **Approach:** Monitor their SMB digitalization reports. Seek expert quotes in their publications. Use their sub-associations as direct channels.

## 4. Accounting Software Partners (Critical Integrations)

### Fortnox ⭐ Top Priority
- **Developer portal:** developer.fortnox.se
- **What:** Sweden's dominant SMB cloud accounting platform — 600,000+ business customers, 500+ app marketplace.
- **Why relevant:** Quotes-to-invoices workflow is natural. Being listed in the Fortnox marketplace = direct access to 600,000 Swedish SMBs. This is the single most important partnership.
- **Approach:** Register as a developer, build the integration, apply for marketplace listing. High effort, highest payoff.

---

### Visma
- **Developer portal:** developer.visma.com
- **What:** Pan-Nordic accounting and ERP platform. Key Swedish SMB product: Visma Spiris (formerly Visma eEkonomi). Structured ISV partner program.
- **Why relevant:** Second largest accounting platform in Sweden. Strong in professional services firms.
- **Approach:** Register at developer.visma.com, build Spiris integration (note Swedish requirements: Moms VAT, Organisationsnummer). Apply to Visma Partner Programme for co-marketing + directory listing.

---

### Bokio
- **Website:** bokio.se
- **What:** Swedish bookkeeping app for very small businesses and sole traders. 80,000+ users. Also operates in UK.
- **Why relevant:** Targets micro-businesses that are underserved by formal proposal tools — natural companion product.
- **Approach:** Reach out to partnership team. Build lightweight integration if API exists. Co-market to their user base.

---

### Hogia
- **Website:** hogia.se
- **What:** Established Swedish accounting and business management software, strong in logistics, transport, HR.
- **Why relevant:** Loyal base of traditional Swedish SMBs — high value when they do adopt new tools.
- **Approach:** Explore API partnership. Target their users with vertical case studies.
